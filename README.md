# Hi, I'm Michał

I study Math & CS at the University of Wrocław and work as an AI engineer. I like problems where algorithms meet messy real-world data, and I like taking them all the way, from the math to something people can actually use.

## Now

- **AI Engineer at Line Control.** I work on online setpoint control for industrial meat-slicing lines. I built a simulator that replays a year of checkweigher data (~43M packs) to benchmark adaptive controllers: robust Kalman filtering, quantile tracking and adaptive conformal inference. I also built a text-to-SQL agent on local LLMs, so plant staff can query production data in plain language.
- **Bachelor's thesis:** adaptive conformal prediction for time series. The update rule is learned while keeping provable coverage guarantees.
- **Before that: Algorithm Engineer at Huawei.** Lower bounds for matrix-vector multiplication (with an RL-driven search), and neural inertial navigation for GPS-free tracking on wearables.

## Projects

- **[GeoGolf](https://github.com/placikk/GeoGolf)** · [▶ play in your browser](https://placikk.github.io/GeoGolf/)<br>
  A geometry puzzle game. You shape conic-section mirrors so that 8 balls fired at once all reach the hole. It runs on a dependency-free engine with exact ray–conic intersection, works offline as a PWA under 100 KB, and has ~700 tests. *TypeScript, Canvas*
- **[Ratlas](https://github.com/placikk/ratlas)** · [▶ live app](https://ratlas.szczury.workers.dev/)<br>
  A citizen-science app for mapping rats in Wrocław. A multimodal model (Gemini) verifies each photo. Anti-cheat uses perceptual hashing, a PostGIS city geofence and Row Level Security, and faces never go public. *React, Supabase, PostGIS*
- **[Polish AI Olympiad 2024](https://github.com/placikk/Polish-AI-Olympiad-2024)**<br>
  Selected solutions from the olympiad, where I was a finalist: neural-network pruning, PGD adversarial attacks, clustering heuristics. *PyTorch*
- **[TreeMetrics](https://github.com/placikk/treemetrics)**<br>
  Approximates 2D point sets with tree metrics (hierarchical clustering) and draws custom geometric visualizations. *NumPy, Shapely*
- **[Competitive-Programming](https://github.com/placikk/Competitive-Programming)**<br>
  My contest solutions from Codeforces, AtCoder, Meta Hacker Cup and more. *C++*
